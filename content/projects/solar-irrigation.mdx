## The problem

ENGR 100 asked for real hardware that moved and sensed — not a slide deck
about what we might build.

## Approach

**Solar vehicle.** Chassis designed and printed in-house, revised whenever the
drivetrain packaging changed.

**Irrigation.** Soil moisture sensors feed a microcontroller that drives the
pump only when a moisture threshold says water is needed — not on a blind
timer.

## Outcome

Two working prototypes and a lot of lessons about packaging, iteration, and
leading the physical side of a student team.
