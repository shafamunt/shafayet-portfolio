## The problem

ENGR 100 asked for real hardware that moved and sensed — not a slide deck
about what we might build.

## Approach

**Solar vehicle.** Designed and 3D printed a custom chassis to optimize weight
and aerodynamics as the drivetrain packaging changed.

**Irrigation.** Prototyped a moisture-sensing system that integrates soil
sensors with a microcontroller for automated fluid control — water only when
the soil asks for it, not on a blind timer.

## Outcome

Two working prototypes and a lot of lessons about packaging, iteration, and
leading the physical side of a student team.
