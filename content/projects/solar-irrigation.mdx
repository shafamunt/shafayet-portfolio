## Problem and outcome

Water the soil only when it is dry — with a solar-fed rail and a valve the MCU
can open. Team demo on an ENGR 100 bench.

## Role

Hardware and prototyping lead: sensor → MCU → valve path, power bring-up, and
3D-printed mounts. Mechanical and presentation work shared across the team.

## System

```
solar panel + battery  →  buck  →  MCU rail
moisture probe  →  threshold  →  solenoid valve → soil
```

Visible on the build: capacitive moisture probe, Uno-class MCU, buck converter,
battery pack, solar panel, and solenoid valve with tubing into the soil cup.

## Limitations

Course prototype, not a field-deployed irrigator. Labels on the annotated image
match the visible hardware in the lab photo. Threshold behavior was used to
decide when to open the valve; hysteresis values are not published here.
