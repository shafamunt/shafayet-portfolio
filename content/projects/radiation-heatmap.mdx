## Problem and outcome

Radiation readings arrive as noisy 2D matrices (mSv/hr). People need heatmaps,
safety zones, a smartwatch crop, and a tumor check on brain scans.

**Outcome:** MATLAB functions `removeNoise`, `heatmap`, `zones`, `WatchDisplay`,
and `detectTumor`.

## Role

- **Course:** ENGR 101, University of Michigan  
- **Spec:** [Project 1 — Dealing with Radiation](http://engr101staff.github.io/engr101.org/projects/project1/specs.html)  
- **My work:** implementing the required MATLAB functions against the course
  autograder and unit tests  

## Pipeline

```
scan_radiation(hour)  →  removeNoise  →  heatmap / zones
GPS + display_settings  →  WatchDisplay
brain scan  →  detectTumor
```
