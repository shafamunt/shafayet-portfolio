## The problem

Raw sensor frames are noisy and hard to read as numbers in a table. The goal
was a pipeline that cleans the signal and makes magnitude obvious visually.

## Approach

**Write the filters.** Custom matrix filters ingest and denoise the frames,
implemented as matrix operations so the math is visible and inspectable.
