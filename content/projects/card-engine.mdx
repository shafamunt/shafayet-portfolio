## Problem and outcome

Euchre needs clear ownership of cards, the pack, and player decisions so a human
seat and an AI seat can share one rules engine.

**Outcome:** a command-line simulator that deals from a pack file, runs trump
selection and trick-taking, and supports Human plus Simple AI players.

## Role

- **Course:** EECS 280, University of Michigan  
- **Spec:** [Project 3 — Euchre](https://eecs280staff.github.io/euchre/)  
- **My work:** `Card`, `Pack`, `Player`, and the `euchre` driver  

## Architecture

```
pack.in  →  Pack  →  deal  →  Player[4] (Human | Simple)
                              deal / trump / play / score
```

## Validation

Unit tests, public compile checks, and system tests against staff
`.out.correct` traces.

## Limitations

Console simulator for the EECS 280 rule set. The cover follows the public
spec’s sample output format so recruiters can see the interaction shape; it is
not a screenshot of my binary.
