## Problem and outcome

Card games stress object-oriented design: rules, state, and players change
independently. I built a Euchre simulation where those pieces stay separable
and an AI seat does not tangle the rules engine.

## Role

Solo project. No team attribution.

## Approach

**Separate concerns.** Players, decks, and game state are their own types. A
human and an AI implement the same player interface.

**Determinism first.** Play is testable so trump selection and card choices can
be debugged without a mysterious RNG.
