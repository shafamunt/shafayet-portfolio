## Why it matters

Race-car boards and personal projects both fail the same way when footprints
are wrong or solder joints are soft. MESH is where those skills get deliberate
practice — the quiet reps behind the MRacing DAQ board path.

## What I do there

PCB design workshops covering schematic capture, footprint selection, and
layout. Hands-on SMT and through-hole soldering and assembly as an authorized
lab user.

## Status

Ongoing — lab practice feeds instrumentation and powertrain board work on
MRacing FSAE, where I hold Level 2 High Voltage (600V) certification. One real
board photo upgrades this page from "skills list" to proof.
