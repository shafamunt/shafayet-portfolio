## Problem and current status

Lap times help only if the car can capture a pass at the start/finish line and
fold that event into telemetry. I am working on the sensing end on
[MRacing FSAE](https://www.mracing.engin.umich.edu/).

**Implemented so far:** breadboard photosensor experimentation and design-review
participation. **Planned:** signal conditioning, a board that latches time, and
publish onto the vehicle bus. I have not implemented the full system.

## Role

Aug 2025 – present · vehicle software / instrumentation support · Level 2 HV
(600V) credential for EV work. Harness strategy and bus conventions are shared
team ownership.

## Target signal path

```
photosensor  →  conditioning  →  MCU edge/timestamp  →  vehicle bus
   [bench today]     [planned]         [planned]           [planned]
```
