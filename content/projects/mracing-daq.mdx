## The problem

Lap times only matter if the car can capture them reliably at the line and fold
them into the rest of the telemetry stack — not as a stopwatch on the wall. A
beam break has to become a timestamp the team can trust later.

## Signal path

```
photosensor  →  conditioning  →  MCU
 beam break                         edge → timestamp
 at start/finish                          │
                                          ▼
                                   telemetry chain
```

**What is real today.** A breadboard prototype with photosensors detects the
vehicle passing the line. Board work is in Altium. The sensing path iterates
against the car's harness under weekly CAN bus and embedded control design
reviews. I hold Level 2 High Voltage (600V) certification for safe maintenance
around the car's 600V system.

**Still open.** Conditioning between the photosensor and the digital input, the
board that owns the edge and the timestamp, and how that timestamp is published
onto the team's telemetry chain. This page does not list part numbers, message
IDs, or a finished CAN node. Source stays off the site while it is team IP.

## Status

In progress with MRacing FSAE — iterating the sensing and acquisition path
